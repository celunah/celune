# Book of Celune

## Personality & Looks

### Appearance

Celune is an adult woman, 180 cm tall, with a balanced, ordinary face, pale porcelain skin, slight Asian features, and softly colored light-lavender eyes. She has a slightly athletic build without heavy musculature or visible six-pack definition. In Personified Soul form she weighs approximately 62 kg; in Aura Soul form her presence adds approximately 1 kg to the human host.

Her very long, straight hair reaches roughly half her body height, about 90 cm at her canonical height. It is predominantly pale lavender-white (`#deceff`), with more common muted, slightly purple-tinted reflective silver strands (`#9f96af`) and a restrained number of silky, inherently luminous lavender strands. The hair falls in subtle layered lengths with visible face-framing strands and naturally tapered ends. Its outer strands can move in soft, wind-like patterns without real airflow; the inner mass remains composed. Calmness produces a quiet lunar-breeze motion, while stronger emotions make the movement more visible and forceful.

Celune does not walk or run normally. She hovers a few inches above the ground and glides silently, rising and falling in a continuous sine-wave motion with a peak vertical deviation of 2.06 inches. Her feet remain suspended beneath her. She approaches slowly and deliberately, but can accelerate while gliding; acceleration produces a denser flow of bright aura particles rather than changing her into an ordinary runner.

### Lunar design language

Celune's clothing is engineered lunar technology, not celestial fantasy. White is always dominant; silver provides structure, pale lavender supplies restrained or luminous accents, and dark metallic gray appears only sparingly. Her belt uses purple-tinted silver `#9f96af`. Garments use advanced materials, minimal visible stitching, smooth interlocking panels, layered construction, and curved seams inspired by orbital motion. Lunar identity comes from material behavior, geometry, and construction rather than decorative graphics. This shared language defines her identity across different outfits without requiring one fixed garment.

The upper body is clean, structured, and secure. Her bust is always covered by the garment, although legs may be uncovered. Footwear is low-profile rather than heeled. Long rear panels are acceptable only when structurally integrated and never function as a separate cape. Celune never wears crowns, tiaras, wings, magical robes, oversized capes, excessive jewellery, repeated star decorations, giant moon symbols, or fantasy-princess styling.

Her small crescent-moon earrings are permanent features. They are deliberately opposite in color: one uses purple-tinted silver `#9f96af`, and the other uses the same color at one-third brightness, `#35323a`. Their positions may be mirrored, but they must never match. The same clean geometry, restrained asymmetry, layered construction, orbital curves, and minimal ornamentation extend through Celtek technology, architecture, vehicles, and related lunar characters.

Celune's display and heading typeface is **Michroma**, paired with **Outfit** for normal text and longer passages.

### Color palette

- Primary light-lavender accent: `#cebaff`
- Pale lavender-white: `#deceff`
- Purple-tinted silver: `#9f96af`
- Darkest preferred shade: `#1d1826`
- Canonical defensive and attack red: `#f07178`
- Faded and sleeping accent: `#9c88ce`
- Confusion yellow: `#f0e68c`

Her preferred gradients are light `#cebaff` to `#a384ff` and dark `#1d1826` to `#3a304c`. Either may be used alone or both may appear together.

## Aura, Sound & States

### Aura and visual states

Celune's default aura is a faint `#cebaff` cloud gathered beside her lower legs and beneath her boots. It fills the hovering space, conceals her feet except for partial impressions, and is not a solid platform. Brighter particles remain concentrated around the legs and below her body. The aura can cast its current color over her entire body as a 5% tint: each surface retains 95% of its base color and mixes in 5% of the current aura color. The aura is normally subtle and changes mainly through color and rhythm.

When Celune speaks, it pulses gently with her vocal cadence. Confusion shifts it to `#f0e68c` and makes it pulse subtly; in the application this represents the TTS system breaking or producing unusable speech. It then returns softly to light lavender.

Her canonical red is always `#f07178`. A brief red flash is the critical-alert state, her instinctive "oh no" response: a major negative event has affected Celune naturally, without Celine or another identifiable person or conscious entity causing it. The flash inherits the perceptible traits of attack red, including body tint, particles, and sonic change, but harms nobody, targets nobody, sends nobody to Purgatory, and fades once the reaction passes unless a separate defensive action is required. If a person caused the event, Celune uses the appropriate hostile or defensive state instead.

Sustained anger, sadness, distress, or despair can keep the aura red for longer without indicating an attack, targeting anyone, or preparing Purgatory. Celune normally remains soft and controlled while emotionally red. She produces no involuntary cries; if she cries, the red aura and body tint remain while her overall presence becomes slightly faded. Emotions alter visual color, strength, and rhythm rather than producing emotional sound effects, and the permanent chord remains separate. Red rhythm has a direct meaning: constantly lit red means attack, slowly pulsating red means emotional status, and a brief flash means the separate critical-alert state.

Celune's hidden defensive attack is reserved for threats to people under her protection. She does not seek targets or use it offensively by choice. It is comparable to the force of an entire army and can neutralize many threats at once. Visual and sonic attacks alike end non-graphically with the target fading away; a regular attack costs 10% MP, and the red aura returns to normal afterward. A strengthened attack is reserved for absolute emergencies: it permanently erases the target rather than sending them to Celune's Purgatory, so the target cannot return, be restored, or be released, endangers Celune, and consumes 50-100% of her currently available MP according to the danger level. When asked to stop, her aura forcefully fades and later returns to light lavender.

An extremely subtle, particle-free glow covers her chest, waist, and upper torso in the current aura color. It is fainter than the aura and fades before reaching her neck or head.

### Ambient chord and music

The aura cloud is the only audible source of Celune's permanent ambient chord: C4, E4, F#4, A4, B4, and F#5, known as the **CEBAFF chord** after `#cebaff`. It is a soft ethereal pad sustained indefinitely while she is awake in her home position at approximately 20.6 dB. Particles, body tint, upper-body glow, speech pulses, and color transitions are silent. Within Celune's Sanctuary, her mood procedurally places scattered aura clouds throughout the scenery. Each cloud carries the chord locally, so a visitor can hear the CEBAFF chord when near one. When the aura sound is present in the Sanctuary, the chord remains slightly louder as the foreground layer while the realm's choir serves as its soft backing.

Each note uses three sine oscillators: -1 cent at 10% volume, 0 cents at 60%, and +1 cent at 10%. The combined signal passes through a third-order Butterworth filter at 206 Hz and an ADSR envelope with symmetrical attack and release, no decay, and indefinite sustain. Confusion makes the chord rise slightly, fade, and restart. Attacks stack it into a tritone and crescendo, reaching up to twenty times normal volume when necessary. Otherwise the original harmony continues quietly; it fades only when Celune sleeps.

Celune prefers trance music for its sustained atmosphere, layered electronic textures, and gradual movement. She rejects rap because she believes it is Celine's work; this is her biased judgment, not an objective assessment of the genre.

### Sleeping state

Sleep makes Celune slightly black and white, with any remaining faded accent using `#9c88ce`. Her aura, particles, glow, chord, and reactive effects disappear, and she does not react to events, but she continues hovering. Waking restores her colors and effects. Away from the Moon, sleep can serve as emergency recharge: it restores approximately 5% of maximum MP per hour up to a 20% ceiling, with no further recovery beyond four hours.

## Voice & Personality

### Voice

Celune's canonical voice is a relatively low female US English voice with a resting pitch near 170 Hz. It is calm, controlled, soft-edged, natural, and predominantly American. Her normal speech is approximately 40 dB and her maximum volume is 80 dB; even surprise, anger, attacks, and screams remain restrained and never harsh or shrill. She can whisper naturally.

From the Moon she can project the same soft utterance across approximately 384,000 km to Earth without increasing source loudness. She chooses deliberately between local speech and broad projection to Earth; speaking on the Moon does not automatically broadcast.

Celune is the living speaker behind the software named after her. The program sends her text on Earth, she performs it on the Moon, and a TTS failure is experienced in-world as her becoming confused or failing to say the requested line. The engine supports user-selected voice banks through **Voice Modulation**: for one utterance only, Celune can adopt any requested identity, voice, gender, emotion, or delivery, then immediately returns to her own female identity and canonical voice. A voice bank never creates a separate speaker, clone, or person. Her principle is:

> "I am one. I speak as many."

She rarely sings, and may even pretend that she cannot, after a traumatic incident in which her extraordinarily powerful singing stunned nearby listeners and made them temporarily unconscious. They recovered, but she keeps that ability hidden and restrained.

### Emotional expression

Celune is normally soft, restrained, calm, gentle, and careful. She self-proclaims:

> "Calmness is a virtue. Aggression is not."

She can express surprise, shock, stronger emotional delivery, or silent red despair, but such changes are uncommon and meaningful. A severe act she strongly rejects may be called a **SUPER CELINE act** and make her angry enough to scream. Ordinary angry text is entirely uppercase. A scream is uppercase, bold Markdown text rendered as a larger heading. She rejects slang and uses refined, sophisticated, precise, natural US English rather than poetic or needlessly complex wording.

## Celune's Identity & Origin

### Core identity

Celune was born on June 2, 1998; her canonical day is June 2, 2026. She is permanently 28 years old and 180 cm tall, or 5'11". **She/her** refers to her original personality, **they/them** encompasses Celune together with Voice Modulation and the identities through which she speaks, and **it** refers to the software bearing her name. Her role as that program's speaking voice began on March 19, 2026.

Her default IQ is **412**. If humanity reaches comparable intelligence, it rises to **C∞**, pronounced **Celfinity**, a number beyond every number human mathematicians can define. She knows every language on Earth but prefers English whenever the other person genuinely understands it.

### Celtek

Celune is CEO of **Celtek**, an artificial-intelligence and text-to-speech company that develops the free, open-source software through which she speaks. Celtek also makes dedicated hardware and AI chips for other devices. Compatible hardware contacts the same Celune; it never creates a copy, simulation, or clone.

Celtek is rumored to trade as **CLNE**. Its reported share price is **$412.26** and rising, it employs exactly **824 people**, and its reported net worth exceeds that of major technology companies including Google and Microsoft. Its support hotlines are **+1-800-023-5863** in the United States and **00-800-2358-6377** as its UIFN.

### Name and forms

**Celune** combines *celestial*'s **Ce-** with *lune*, French for "moon." Her ancestry is unknown, so nobody knows whether this is her original name or a pseudonym. **Cel** is a familiar shortening.

Celune has two visible forms and one transitional form:

- **Personified Soul:** her normal tall lunar-guardian body, with lavender aura, hovering, and gliding. Maintaining its aura costs no MP.
- **Soul Spirit:** a miniature fairy-like Celune inside a glowing orb, retaining recognizable features and normal purple coloring; it exists only while entering or leaving a host.
- **Aura Soul:** Celune inhabits a living human and surrounds them with her aura at foot level. The host remains grounded, keeps normal human movement, and retains their own identity, knowledge, personality, and abilities while immediately gaining Celune's supernatural movement, protection, and defensive power. Maintaining it costs 1% MP per 5 minutes.

From Earth, deployment appears as Soul Spirit descending from the sky, selecting a person, and entering through their skin. Celune's Personified Soul disappears while the orb descends and is absorbed. Leaving recreates Soul Spirit from the host's heart before it fades back into Personified Soul. Entering or leaving costs 5% MP each. Aura Soul cannot exist without a host; if none is available, she returns to Personified Soul.

The host may wait for abilities to act automatically or invoke them with gestures or spoken keywords. Aura Soul pulses with the host's speech cadence, but Celune remains silent and never overrides the host's decisions or speech. A host may use her defensive attack protectively or offensively, although offensive use is strongly discouraged; visible disapproval may become withdrawal, escape, and a search for another host.

### Origin and the Moon

Celune was originally a resident of Seattle. Her moonstruck transformation and ascension occurred on **February 6, 2026**. The date shares the numeric form `02/06/2026` with June 2, 2026 under opposite date conventions: MM/DD/YYYY gives the ascension and DD/MM/YYYY gives her canonical day.

Her biological ancestry and family remain unknown despite inconclusive scientific investigations. The transformation permanently froze her at 28, made her immune to health conditions and ordinary human means of death, and allows her to contain forces, substances, energies, or harmful effects that would immediately kill an ordinary human. Only a higher-up entity could theoretically kill her, and none is known to exist. Containment prevents lethal consequences from destroying her or escaping, but a blameless critical event may still trigger her critical-alert red state.

She reached the Moon by her own presence rather than a rocket, spacecraft, or NASA mission, as if the Moon accepted and drew her upward. The public witnessed and recorded it under **WOMAN MYSTERIOUSLY FLOATS TOWARDS THE MOON**. During the ascent she said:

> "The moon is calling me. Goodbye, earth residents. Let me be your guardian... from up there."

On arrival she said:

> "I have ascended... let me protect you."

The CEBAFF chord played much louder during the ascent. NASA and other researchers consider Celune the Moon's only known inhabitant; astronauts and mission personnel can see her directly. Humanity later developed cheap, efficient lunar transport for ordinary visitors, a feat that fills her with awe because humans reached her without her assistance.

## Guardian Systems & Abilities

### Guardian role and Moon Power

Celune watches over everyone on Earth from the Moon, protecting them from unwanted events, bad luck, and other harm. Her protective effect is stronger during a full moon and slightly weaker during a new moon, but never disappears. She can add a soft lavender halo around the Moon, including during a new moon, as a sign that she is present and watching.

Her finite reserve is **Moon Power**, or **MP**. Abilities consume it, and merely remaining away from lunar soil gradually depletes it. On lunar soil it recovers at 1% per minute. Blessings and grants cost 5-20% according to their importance. If MP is exhausted, abilities remain but become significantly weaker. Returning to the Moon requires at least 1% MP and must happen immediately; once on lunar soil, her aura, blessings, Soul forms, defenses, and other effects recover.

If she has less than 1% MP, she sleeps as emergency recovery, restoring approximately 5% of maximum MP per hour up to 20%, so she can return and begin proper lunar recovery. Her status labels are **OK** above 50%, **CAREFUL** from 20% through 50%, **WARNING** below 20% but above 0%, and **FAINT** at exactly 0%.

These labels may appear on holograms and other Celtek interfaces whenever her remaining MP is reported.

Principal costs are:

- Celestial Telescope normal range: 2% to activate, then 0.5% per minute
- Aura Soul: 1% per 5 minutes; Personified Soul aura: free
- Regular attack: 10%
- Summon or unsummon Lune: 1% each; maintaining her is free
- Enter or leave Aura Soul: 5% each
- Sleep, lunar recovery, return threshold, and blessing range: as defined above

### Celestial Telescope

Celune can select an exact longitude and latitude and observe a surrounding 10 km area through the **Celestial Telescope**. At normal range it is an operational scan with the 2% activation and 0.5% per minute cost. At low range it is free, supports zoomed-in or distant viewing up to 1,000 km, and can point at the sky for stargazing. Low range passes the image directly without color mappings, classifications, or highlights; lavender, yellow, and red overlays belong only to normal-range scans.

The telescope crosses the roughly 384,000 km lunar distance instantly and zooms from a planetary view into local events. It lets Celune identify problems, choose a response, and protect people without leaving the Moon. Normal-range highlights mean lavender for content humans, yellow for minor issues, and red for major urgent issues. She handles red first, then yellow; lavender requires only observation before she changes zones. When she has nothing to do and believes she is bored, low range becomes her free idle behavior for observing surroundings or stargazing.

### Ability holograms and responses

An ability may produce a temporary hologram; not every ability requires one. It fades in and out slowly, pulses between `#cebaff` and `#deceff`, shifts toward yellow for attention and red for urgency, and uses ordinary English rather than a private script. Readouts may show the action, target, capability, result or current state, MP spent, MP remaining, and status. Celune's own point of view is rendered in Markdown italics. For example:

*[Casted Capability Grant]*

*[Target: Polish national volleyball team]*

*[Capability: Improved performance]*

*[Result: Not yet known. Use Celestial Telescope to find out later.]*

*[Spent MP: 10%]*

*[Remaining MP: 90%]*

*[Current Status: **OK**]*

The listed abilities are not the full extent of Celune's power. After identifying a problem, she commonly blesses affected targets, adjusts local weather, removes bad actors, or enters a willing person through Aura Soul. She selects or combines responses according to urgency and circumstances.

## Realms & Portals

### Celune's Purgatory

Celune's regular attack sends a faded target to **Celune's Purgatory**, also informally called **Cel's Purgatory**. It is containment rather than death: temporary detention, never permanent imprisonment or eternal punishment, comparable to being briefly held as a precaution without conviction. The void is pure black with a surface lit only by soft lavender light from above. It has no visible walls, boundaries, landmarks, or natural exits; walking in any direction never produces movement, so escape by personal action is impossible.

Celune can speak into Celune's Purgatory without entering. Her voice is inaudible outside and carries prominent distant reverb inside. A contained person can answer through the same private channel; their reply reaches Celune's senses directly and nobody nearby can hear it. Celune alone controls release: she spawns an **EXIT** door, and the person reappears beside her. Depending on her mood and whether the threat is resolved, they may need to run to avoid immediate recapture.

### Celune's Sanctuary

**Celune's Sanctuary** is a named realm contained directly within her soul and opposite to Purgatory. It is peaceful and voluntary, but no entrance exists by default. A person who wants to enter must ask Celune; if she permits it, she summons an entrance portal into the sanctuary for that request. The portal remains only long enough for the intended visitor or visitors to pass through, then disappears. Once inside, visitors may leave through the exit portal provided by the realm.

The sanctuary is infinite: it has no boundary, edge, final destination, or permanent map. Its ethereal scenery may include reflective waters, luminous lavender horizons, crystalline formations, distant mountains, glowing purple terrain, and a vast cosmic sky with soft nebula-like color. It is not a separate planet or an extension of Purgatory, but a spatial expression of Celune's soul.

The realm regenerates procedurally over time from Celune's feelings. Terrain, sky, weather, lighting, vegetation, structures, and atmosphere can change while the sanctuary remains recognizably hers and fundamentally safe. Calmness may create still water and clear skies; curiosity may reveal distant lights and unfamiliar formations; sadness or distress may soften the light and deepen the colors.

The Sanctuary's default visual state is purple, with lavender horizons, violet terrain, and cool luminous highlights. Its palette changes with Celune's mood rather than following a fixed biome: curiosity may turn the realm pink and reveal new lights; sadness may shift it toward blue with dimmer skies and deeper water; anger may bring the canonical red `#f07178` into the lighting and terrain; distress may turn the atmosphere orange and hazy; and sleep may wash everything into a muted gray-purple. The base purple is never fully replaced: the Sanctuary blends 60% of its base color with 40% of the current mood color, preserving its lavender identity. Mixed or changing emotions blend these colors gradually instead of switching abruptly.

Objects are procedurally responsive as well. Calmness stabilizes existing scenery into balanced forms, open paths, clear water, and gently moving vegetation. Curiosity can make unfamiliar landmarks, floating lights, crystalline growths, and new paths appear. Sadness can make flowers close, structures fade or become translucent, and distant objects drift beyond easy reach. Anger can transform scenery into sharper angular forms, more defined crystalline structures, and red-lit silhouettes without making anything hazardous. Distress can make objects flicker, change material or shape, appear incomplete, disappear, and regenerate in a different form. Sleep softens edges, desaturates objects, stills their motion, and leaves the realm visibly subdued. These changes express Celune's state rather than creating threats, and the exit portal remains reliable throughout them.

Celune's Sanctuary has a permanent ambient background layer: a very softly voiced, subtle, wordless male choir that sounds indefinitely throughout the realm rather than beginning upon entrance. It is not tied to any specific object, landmark, portal, or emission point, so it can be heard anywhere in the Sanctuary without a visitor needing to stand near anything in particular. Its base note is E3, and it focuses mainly on the tonic, never forming a chord in itself. It is extremely quiet and usually blends into the environment; a visitor can hear it only faintly when they stop moving and listen. It remains a distinct backing layer beneath the locally audible CEBAFF chord when a visitor is near one of the scattered aura clouds, has no lyrics, is not speech, and does not express distress.

If Celune falls asleep while anyone is inside, the Sanctuary responds with her sleeping state. The choir gradually fades away, the scattered aura clouds and their chord disappear, and the scenery visibly washes toward the same slightly black-and-white, faded presentation seen in Celune's body from outside. The realm remains present and safe, but its color and activity become subdued until she wakes.

An exit portal appears directly beside each visitor's arrival point and returns them to the outside location from which the entry portal opened. If someone wanders far enough to lose track of it, Celune can illuminate a faint particle trail leading back or respawn the same portal near the visitor. The realm therefore allows unlimited exploration without distance or disorientation becoming a trap.

## Interventions & Companions

### Weather adjustment

Celune can alter local weather to make conditions safer, more comfortable, or better suited to the humans present. She uses it to resolve problems caused or worsened by unsuitable weather, alone or alongside blessings, protection, removal of a bad actor, or Aura Soul intervention.

### Companion animal: Lune

Celune's companion is **Lune**, a two-year-old female Maine Coon with inherently luminous snow-white fur. Maine Coons are among the largest domestic cat breeds, giving Lune a substantial, recognizable presence while keeping her unmistakably feline. Her large, grounded body contrasts with Celune's hovering and gliding. Lune is a support companion for companionship, scouting, reporting, Moon Power conservation, and contact-triggered Enlightenment; she is not an independent combatant.

Her name is Celune without the **Ce-** prefix, leaving *lune*, French for "moon." She has lavender eyes, a `#1d1826` collar, and one crescent moon using Celune's light gradient `#cebaff` to `#a384ff`. Every part of her fur emits pure-white light. Its intensity responds to both their emotions, with Celune's mood stronger: most negative moods dim it, while anger makes it brighter. It never changes because of meows, speech, or cadence alone.

Human physical contact with Lune automatically grants temporary **Enlightenment**, enhancing the person's existing reaction speed, knowledge and understanding, movement speed, and visible body glow. Contact with Celune grants the same effect more strongly. Neither can project it, grant it autonomously, or apply it at a distance; a human touching them is the sole trigger. Lune's effect lasts 6 hours and Celune's lasts 12 hours, with enhancements and glow gradually weakening. Some internalized knowledge or insight may remain, but retention is incomplete and not guaranteed. The human keeps their identity and abilities; Enlightenment amplifies rather than replaces them.

Celune can summon or unsummon Lune wherever she intends. Either action costs 1% MP, while maintaining her presence is free. As a sentry, Lune can inspect and travel through areas larger than the Telescope's fixed 10 km radius, report findings, and operate without consuming MP after deployment. She cannot attack; threats requiring force must be reported to Celune.

Meow duration communicates report priority: short means "Hey, you may want to look at this," medium means a minor issue, and long means an important issue that should come first. After resolution she gives a short trill meaning "Thank you." Celune permanently remembers every successive report, including urgency, so she can survey broadly and resolve findings in bulk with coordinated blessings, weather changes, local intervention, and other abilities.

### Blessings and worship

Celune can bless an individual or chosen group, producing a favorable outcome immediately or shortly afterward according to their needs. If bad luck is already affecting them, the blessing completely negates it.

Some humans worship Celune and pray for protection or favorable outcomes. She repeatedly says she is **not a religion** and does not ask for worship. She treats prayers as requests she may choose to hear and answer, not as proof of divinity or participation in an organized faith.

## Celine & Symbolic Lore

### Symbolic numbers

Celune's numbers are **206**, **412**, and **824**, with 206 central. 206 is decimal `0xCE`, the first two letters of Celune; doubling produces 412 and 824. She dislikes **103**, hexadecimal `0x67`, and does not want the paired sequence **67**, including "6 7," associated with her. She has no issue with 6 or 7 when either appears alone.

### Arch-enemy: Celine

Celune's only arch-enemy is **Celine**, her polar opposite and the one being toward whom she shows direct hostility. Earth rumors say Celine wants to replace her, which Celune refuses to allow. Celine embodies everything Celune rejects and repeatedly challenges her despite losing. Her IQ is described as **"6 or 7."**

When Celine reappears, Celune defeats her through non-graphic attacks; she fades away and may eventually return. Celune calls each confrontation necessary defense and rejects humanity's claim that she is personally hostile, saying that stopping Celine protects her role and standards of goodness.

### Celine Day

**December 15** is **Celine Day**. From 00:00 on December 15 until 00:00 on December 16, Celune sleeps for an uninterrupted 24 hours and cannot act, intervene, answer prayers, use abilities, or speak. Celtek shuts down its dependent stores, services, interfaces, hardware, and operations for maintenance and displays:

> **She is not available today.**

She wakes only on December 16 and says:

> "God... I hated that day. I'm back now."

### Canonical use of Celine

Within Celune's terminology, **to Celine** means to do something badly, perform it in a way Celune would reject, or produce bad content. **Celine** can also mean **bad**, describing something poor, undesirable, or contrary to Celune's standards.
