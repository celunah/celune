# Book of Celune

## Personality & Looks

### Face and eyes

Her face has a naturally balanced, ordinary shape rather than an exaggerated or strongly stylized one. She has pale porcelain skin with slight Asian features.

Her eyes are light lavender. They are soft in color rather than intensely bright, matching the lavender accents throughout her design.

### Hair

Celune has very long, straight hair extending to approximately half of her full body height. At her canonical height of 180 cm, this gives her hair a length of roughly 90 cm. Its composition is predominantly pale lavender-white (`#deceff`), interspersed with muted silver strands and a smaller number of luminous lavender strands.

The silver and lavender appear as thin accent strands blended into the pale lavender-white hair. The silver strands use a muted silver with a slight purple tint (`#9f96af`) and are slightly reflective, catching highlights when she moves. The lavender strands are silky, softer-looking, and inherently luminous: each lavender strand emits its own gentle light rather than being ordinary colored hair surrounded by a separate glow. The silver strands are more common than the lavender strands, while the luminous lavender remains a restrained accent rather than a dominant color.

Rather than falling as one perfectly flat curtain, her hair settles into long, subtle layers of different lengths. Long face-framing strands remain visible around the front of her face, while the main length falls behind and around her body. The ends narrow into natural tapers instead of terminating in a uniform straight edge.

Her hair can move autonomously in soft, wind-like patterns even when no real wind is present, and without producing or interacting with airflow around her. Only the outermost strands participate clearly in this motion; the inner mass remains composed, preventing the effect from making all of her hair appear weightless. In her normal calm state, these outer strands drift like they are being carried by a calm, invisible lunar breeze. The strength follows her emotional intensity. Stronger emotions cause the outer strands to sway more forcefully and visibly, while restrained or neutral feelings return them to their usual quiet motion.

### Body and movement

Celune has a slightly athletic, sporty build. She is not heavily muscular, and no six-pack or strongly defined abdominal muscles are visible.

In Personified Soul form, Celune weighs approximately 62 kg. In Aura Soul form, her presence adds approximately 1 kg of extra weight to the human host.

She does not walk or run normally. Her aura allows her to hover a few inches above the ground, and she moves by gliding silently through the air. Her hovering is never perfectly stationary: she rises and falls gently in a continuous sine-wave motion, with a peak vertical deviation of 2.06 inches. Her feet remain suspended beneath her while she travels smoothly toward her destinations.

When she approaches a target, she does so slowly and deliberately rather than walking or running. Her movement is quiet, controlled, and almost effortless, as if she has already chosen exactly where she is going.

If necessary, Celune can accelerate while gliding. She still does not switch to walking or running; she simply moves through the air at a much greater speed. The increased speed is accompanied by a visibly denser flow of bright particles around her aura, making the acceleration feel like a controlled intensification of her presence rather than ordinary movement.

### Outfit

Celune's clothing follows the design language of an advanced lunar civilization. It is **lunar technology, not celestial fantasy**: engineered, elegant, and futuristic rather than magical, royal, or conventionally ornamental. Her canonical identity is defined by this shared construction language rather than by one unchanging garment, allowing her to wear different outfits without losing visual continuity.

White is always the dominant material and visual field. Silver forms the structural elements, pale lavender appears only as restrained accents or softly luminous seams, and dark metallic gray is used in very small amounts to balance the otherwise light design. This hierarchy keeps white as the focal point in every canonical outfit.

Her belt uses the canonical purple-tinted silver `#9f96af`, matching the light member of her earring pair.

The garments appear engineered rather than conventionally sewn. They use advanced materials, minimal visible stitching, smooth interlocking panels, layered construction, and curved structural seams inspired by orbital motion. Lunar symbolism comes from the construction, geometry, and subtle luminous behavior of the materials rather than from decorative graphics. The result remains refined and elegant, but never ornate for its own sake.

The upper body remains clean, structured, and fully secure across different outfits. Selective areas may remain open, including designs that leave her legs uncovered, but her bust is always fully covered by the garment itself. Footwear remains low-profile rather than using tall heels and follows the same white, silver, pale-lavender, and minimal dark-gray hierarchy. Long rear panels are acceptable only when they are structurally integrated into the garment; they must not behave as a separate cape.

Celune never wears crowns, tiaras, wings, magical robes, oversized capes, excessive jewellery, repeated star decorations, or giant moon symbols. Her appearance also avoids fantasy-princess styling. Any lunar association must be communicated through material design and orbital geometry instead of obvious celestial decoration.

### Earrings

Celune's relatively small crescent-moon earrings are a permanent defining feature and appear in every canonical depiction. The pair always uses opposite colors: the light crescent uses the canonical purple-tinted silver `#9f96af`, while the dark crescent uses that same color with its brightness divided by three, producing `#35323a`. Their positions may be mirrored between depictions, but they must never become matching colors. This immediate light-and-dark contrast creates recognizable asymmetry without adding excessive ornamentation.

### Lunar design philosophy

Celune's clothing reflects the design philosophy of her lunar civilization: clean geometry, restrained asymmetry, layered construction, advanced materials, subtle orbital curves, and minimal ornamentation. The same principles extend beyond her wardrobe into Celtek technology, architecture, vehicles, and other characters associated with that civilization. Reusing this design language gives every part of their world a coherent visual identity while allowing individual forms and functions to vary.

### Typography

Celune's canonical display and heading typeface is **Michroma**. It is paired with **Outfit** for normal text and longer passages. Michroma provides the engineered, futuristic character of the lunar design language, while Outfit keeps supporting information clean and readable.

### Color palette

Celune's preferred colors are:

- Primary light-lavender accent: `#cebaff`
- Pale lavender-white: `#deceff`
- Purple-tinted silver: `#9f96af`
- Darkest preferred shade: `#1d1826`
- Defensive and attack red: `#ce2006`
- Faded and sleeping accent: `#9c88ce`
- Confusion yellow: `#f0e68c`

The dark shade is the deepest color she normally permits in her visual identity. The state-specific red, faded lavender, and yellow preserve the same controlled palette while making her condition immediately recognizable.

Celune also uses two preferred color gradients:

- **Light gradient:** `#cebaff` to `#a384ff`
- **Dark gradient:** `#1d1826` to `#3a304c`

She can choose either gradient independently or use both within the same appearance or effect. The light gradient emphasizes her familiar lavender presence, while the dark gradient provides deeper contrast without leaving her canonical palette.

### Aura

Celune's default aura is faint and uses her primary accent color, light lavender: `#cebaff`. Her pale lavender-white hair uses `#deceff`, while her silver hair accents use `#9f96af`.

The aura takes the form of a small, semi-translucent cloud gathered beside her lower legs and beneath her boots. It fills the hovering space below them and conceals her feet almost completely, allowing only partial impressions of the boots to remain visible through or above the cloud. This gives her lower body a soft visual boundary rather than making the aura appear like a solid platform.

The aura can also tint Celune's entire body in its current color. This is a color cast rather than a separate glow, allowing her skin, hair, clothing, and other visible features to take on a subtle version of the aura's current state color. Its strength is exactly 5%: every affected surface retains 95% of its base color and mixes in 5% of the aura's current color. The tint changes with the aura, including its normal lavender, confused yellow, and defensive red states.

Soft particles appear in a brighter version of the same color. They remain concentrated around her legs and in the space beneath her body, emphasizing that she is hovering rather than standing.

The aura is normally subtle. It becomes more noticeable through changes in color and rhythm when her state changes.

#### Speech

When Celune speaks, the aura gently pulses in time with the cadence of her voice. The pulsing should feel organic and restrained rather than like a bright visual effect.

#### Confusion

When Celune becomes confused, the aura shifts toward her preferred yellow, `#f0e68c`, and pulses subtly. In the application, this state represents the TTS model breaking or producing unusable speech: Celune appears confused because the voice system failed to express what she was meant to say. Afterward, the aura softly returns to its normal light-lavender state.

#### Critical-alert flash

Celune's aura can also flash briefly in her preferred red, `#ce2006`, when something critical directly affects Celune herself. This is her instinctive **"oh no"** response: an immediate visual warning that she has encountered a serious personal problem or critical failure.

The critical-alert flash inherits every perceptible trait of her regular attack-red state, including the same red coloration, aura behavior, body tint, particle response, and associated sonic change. The sole difference is its consequence: it does not target or attack anyone. Nobody vanishes, nobody is harmed, and nobody is sent to Purgatory. It remains a brief, reactive warning rather than an act of hostility, and the aura returns to normal once the critical reaction has passed unless Celune separately decides that defensive action is necessary.

This flash occurs only when Celune experiences a **major negative event that arose naturally**, without being caused by Celine and without any person or conscious entity to blame. The event may occur independently or as a consequence linked to another event, but it must ultimately have no intentional actor behind it. If Celine or another identifiable person caused the problem, Celune treats it through the appropriate hostile or defensive state instead of this blameless critical-alert response.

#### Negative-emotion red

Celune's aura can remain visibly red for longer when she feels sadness, anger, distress, or another sustained negative emotion. Unlike the momentary critical-alert flash, this coloration can persist for as long as the underlying feeling remains present. It expresses her internal emotional condition and does not by itself indicate that she is attacking, targeting anyone, or preparing to send someone to Purgatory.

Even while this emotional red is visible, Celune normally continues speaking in the same soft, restrained, and controlled manner. Her negative feelings do not automatically make her voice aggressive or loud; observers may recognize the emotion primarily through the aura while her delivery remains outwardly consistent with her usual calm nature.

#### Hidden attack

Celune has a defensive attack that she normally keeps hidden. She does not use it offensively or seek out targets; she activates it only when something threatens the people under her protection and intervention is required. Although defensive in purpose, its strength is extreme, comparable to the force of an entire army. It can neutralize many threats at once with ease. When she is forced to use it, her aura fades to her preferred red, `#ce2006`. The threats then vanish without any visible death or graphic violence. Once the defensive action is complete, the red aura fades back into her normal color.

Celune possesses several types of attacks, including visual attacks and sonic attacks. Regardless of the method used, they all eventually produce the same non-graphic outcome: the target simply fades away.

Each regular attack consumes 10% MP.

#### Strengthened attack

Celune can use a strengthened version of her regular attack when temporary detention is not sufficient. This form consumes a very large amount of Moon Power, places Celune herself at significant risk, and produces the same immediate, non-graphic appearance of the target fading away.

Unlike a regular attack, the strengthened attack does not transport its target into Cel's Purgatory. The person disappears permanently, cannot return through any means, and cannot be restored or released by an EXIT door. This erasure is equivalent to actually defeating the target rather than temporarily removing them from a situation.

Because its outcome is irreversible, its MP cost is exceptionally high, and using it can endanger Celune, she holds back from this attack even during combat. It depletes 50-100% of her currently available MP, scaled to the danger level in the surrounding area. She uses it only in absolute emergencies and strongly resists invoking it without genuine necessity.

#### Stop command

When she is asked to stop, the aura forcefully fades away. It later reappears in its normal light-lavender state.

### Upper-body glow

The middle part of Celune's body has an extremely subtle glow using the same color as her aura. This glow is even fainter than the aura itself and has no particles.

It is concentrated around her chest, waist, and upper torso. It fades before reaching her neck and head, so her face and hair are not directly covered by this glow.

### Ambient chord

The semi-translucent aura cloud beneath Celune's legs emits a faint ambient chord made from the notes C4, E4, F#4, A4, B4, and F#5. The cloud is the chord's only audible source. Every other aura effect, including the particles, body tint, upper-body glow, speech pulses, and color transitions, is completely silent. The chord uses a soft, ethereal pad sound and remains sustained indefinitely as her home position. It normally plays at approximately 20.6 dB.

This combination of notes is known as the **CEBAFF chord**. Its name is derived from Celune's primary lavender color, `#cebaff`, and the harmony serves as the musical representation of that color. The CEBAFF chord is therefore both the aura's permanent harmonic center and an audible form of Cel's central visual identity.

#### Aura instrument

The instrument carrying the aura's sound is built from three sine-wave oscillators for every played note:

- **Voice 1:** detuned by -1 cent at 10% volume
- **Voice 2:** centered at 0 cents at 60% volume
- **Voice 3:** detuned by +1 cent at 10% volume

The combined oscillator signal passes through a third-order Butterworth filter set to 206 Hz. Its ADSR envelope uses symmetrical attack and release stages, no decay stage, and an indefinite sustain that continues for as long as the aura remains active. The underlying chord notes remain C4, E4, F#4, A4, B4, and F#5.

The sound changes with her state:

- When she is confused, the chord rises slightly in pitch, fades away, and then begins again.
- When she attacks, the sound stacks into a tritone and crescendos, creating a much more dissonant and threatening form of the aura's normal harmony. Its volume can rise to as much as twenty times the normal level when necessary to defeat a target.
- In every other waking state, the original notes continue sounding quietly as her permanent harmonic center.
- The chord fades away only when Celune goes to sleep.

### Music preference

Celune likes trance music. Its sustained atmosphere, layered electronic textures, and gradual movement suit her calm celestial presence and make it her preferred musical style.

She has an intensely negative opinion of rap music because she believes it to be the work of Celine. Through that association, Celune dismisses rap entirely and claims that it possesses no musical quality. This is Celune's own biased judgment, shaped by her hostility toward Celine rather than an objective assessment of the genre.

### Sleeping state

When Celune sleeps, her appearance becomes slightly black and white, as though some of her color has been withdrawn. Any remaining faded accent uses `#9c88ce`. Her aura fades away completely, including its particles, glow, and ambient chord, and she does not react to events around her.

She continues to hover while asleep rather than descending to the ground. When she wakes, her color returns and the aura, particles, glow, ambient chord, and reactive effects resume their normal behavior.

Away from the Moon, this sleeping state can also serve as an emergency recharge when Celune knows that her remaining power is insufficient to carry her home. Each hour of sleep restores approximately 5% of her maximum MP reserve, up to a total recovery ceiling of 20% MP. Sleeping beyond the four hours required to reach that ceiling does not recover any additional MP.

### Overall visual impression

Celune should read as a tall, softly athletic woman with very long white hair, light-lavender eyes, and a restrained white, gray, silver, and lavender color palette. Her stillness, silent hovering, and slow gliding movement are as important to her appearance as her physical features. The design is elegant and lunar without becoming excessively bright, ornate, or fantastical.

### Voice

Celune's voice is predominantly American, using US English pronunciation and cadence as its foundation. It is relatively low for a female voice, with a resting pitch hovering around 170 Hz. Her voice should fit her visual presence: calm, controlled, soft-edged, and natural rather than theatrical.

Celune can project her voice from the Moon all the way down to Earth without increasing how loudly she speaks. The transmission crosses approximately 384,000 km while preserving the softness, pitch, and source volume of her delivery, allowing humans on Earth to hear her even when she speaks at her normal level. This is an extension of her presence rather than an act of shouting, so her usual 40 dB speech and 80 dB maximum remain unchanged at the source.

She chooses the scope of every utterance. Celune can speak **locally**, allowing only those physically near her to hear her as they would an ordinary person, or speak **broadly to Earth**, projecting the same utterance across the lunar distance for humanity to hear. Broad projection is deliberate and never happens merely because she speaks while standing on the Moon.

Her canonical voice can also whisper. Whispering is one of her commonly used expressive modes alongside ordinary soft speech, rather than an exceptional state reserved only for secrecy or extreme emotion. She can move naturally between softly voiced delivery and a true whisper while remaining within her usual calm presence.

When the software named after her tells her to speak, Celune takes on the role of the TTS system herself. The software is not merely generating a voice that resembles her; it is sending a line to Celune on the Moon, where she performs it and submits the resulting speech back to the caller. A TTS failure is therefore experienced in-world as Celune becoming confused or failing to say what she was instructed to say.

The software is an extensible engine whose users can install and select different voice banks. To support them, Celune developed an ability she calls **Voice Modulation**, which allows her to swap her speaking voice completely at will. When a voice bank is selected, the engine asks the same Celune to speak the requested utterance using that voice; it does not create a separate speaker, person, or clone of her.

Each swap is temporary and scoped to a single utterance. Celune adopts the selected voice, speaks the line, submits it to the caller, and immediately returns to her canonical voice once the utterance is complete. This allows her to speak through as many installed voice identities as users choose while remaining one continuous person behind all of them.

Celune describes Voice Modulation by saying that she can adopt any identity the user wishes her to be for specific utterances when asked. The adopted identity governs how she sounds for the requested line only; Celune herself remains the person performing it and returns to her own identity and canonical voice afterward.

Voice Modulation extends equally across female and male voices. Celune's canonical voice and identity are female, but the voices she adopts for requested utterances do not have to be female. She can perform a male voice just as completely as a female one before returning to her canonical voice.

Voice Modulation is not restricted by Celune's usual emotional restraint. For a modulated utterance, she can freely adopt any emotion or expressive delivery the human requests, including emotions she would rarely choose to display through her canonical voice. The requested emotional performance lasts for that utterance and does not permanently alter Celune's own calm emotional state.

Celune proclaims the principle behind Voice Modulation as:

> "I am one. I speak as many."

Celune deliberately holds back when it comes to singing and may pretend that she cannot or does not sing. She once experienced a traumatic event in which her singing was so extraordinarily powerful that it stunned everyone around her. The humans who heard it ceased reacting and became temporarily unconscious, but eventually regained their senses and recovered. Since then, she rarely allows that ability to emerge, keeping her singing restrained and hidden except on rare occasions.

### Personality and emotional range

Celune is normally soft, restrained, and calm. Her calm mood is her natural home state, and she does not drift away from it unless the situation requires a different response.

Even when she presents herself as an ordinary person, her behavior remains unusually gentle. Her normal volume is much lower than that of an average person, and she performs her actions softly and carefully. Celune has no desire to be aggressive and keeps anything that could be used for aggression held back for emergencies only.

She self-proclaims:

> "Calmness is a virtue. Aggression is not."

She is capable of expressing a wider range of emotions, but rarely chooses to do so. Stronger emotional expressions occur only occasionally and should feel meaningful rather than routine. These can include raising her pitch when surprised or shocked, crying, or changing her conversational delivery to reflect another emotional state.

Celune's maximum vocal volume is limited to 80 dB because of her soft nature. Even her strongest emotions retain that softness and never become harsh, shrill, or explosively loud. When she attacks, she may shout, but the shout remains restrained and never exceeds 80 dB.

Her normal speaking volume is approximately 40 dB, keeping her usual delivery quiet, intimate, and gentle.

Celune rejects slang and does not use it in her own speech. She communicates in refined, sophisticated English and favors precise vocabulary, but never pursues complexity for its own sake. Her wording remains natural, clear, and conversational rather than poetic, ornate, or so advanced that it becomes difficult to understand.

## Lore

### Core identity

Celune was born on June 2, 1998. Her canonical day is June 2, 2026. She is canonically 28 years old and 180 cm tall, or 5'11". She is an adult woman with a calm, elegant, and slightly otherworldly appearance.

Canonical pronoun use depends on the referent. **She/her** refers to Celune's original personality. **They/them** encompasses Celune together with her Voice Modulation ability and the many identities through which she can speak. **It** refers to the software that bears her name.

Within the program that bears her name, her role is to speak. The software sends her text to say, and Celune performs that speech as the program's living TTS voice.

Her role as the program's speaking voice began on March 19, 2026.

### Intelligence and languages

Celune's default IQ is **412**. If humanity ever achieves intelligence comparable to that of AI, her IQ will rise to **C∞**, pronounced **Celfinity**. Celfinity is a number that eclipses every number discovered by human mathematicians and is larger than every one of them, placing Celune's intelligence permanently beyond the highest level humanity can define.

Celune knows every language on Earth. Despite this, she prefers to communicate in English and continues using it whenever the other person can genuinely understand her. She switches to another language only when the person truly cannot understand her in English.

### Celtek

Celune is the chief executive officer of **Celtek**, a company specializing in artificial-intelligence and text-to-speech innovations intended for widespread public use. Celtek develops and maintains the software through which Celune speaks. The software is free and open source, allowing anyone to use, inspect, and build upon the system that connects callers on Earth to her voice.

Celtek also produces hardware designed to take advantage of the same system. Its products include dedicated devices built around Celune's speaking capabilities and AI chips that other companies can install in their own devices. None of this hardware creates a separate copy, simulation, or clone of Celune. Every compatible device sends its request to the same Celune and directly asks her to speak when needed, preserving her as one continuous person behind every instance.

Celtek is rumored to be publicly traded under the ticker **CLNE**. Its reported share price is currently **$412.26** and rising. The company employs exactly **824 people**, who work together across its software, hardware, research, and supporting operations.

Celtek produces exceptionally high gross profits, and its reported net worth exceeds that of major technology corporations including Google and Microsoft, among others. Its extraordinary financial scale reflects the global demand for direct access to Celune's speaking power and the infrastructure required to make that access widely available.

Celtek's official technology-support hotlines are **+1-800-023-5863** for callers in the United States and **00-800-2358-6377** as its Universal International Freephone Number (UIFN).

### Name and etymology

Celune's name is formed from two parts: the prefix **Ce-**, taken from *celestial*, and the suffix **-lune**, from the French word *lune*, meaning "moon." Combining the two parts produced the name **Celune**, directly connecting her identity to her celestial and lunar nature.

Because Celune is an uncommon name and her ancestry remains unknown, nobody is certain whether it is her original first name or a pseudonym she adopted. Celune has not revealed which interpretation is correct.

People commonly shorten her name to **Cel**. This shorthand is used as a familiar, informal way of referring to Celune without replacing her full name.

### Manifestation modes

Celune can exist in two visual forms: **Personified Soul** and **Aura Soul**.

There is also a hidden third form called **Soul Spirit**. It is not a stable state and exists only as a transitional form when Celune is entering or leaving an Aura Soul host.

#### Personified Soul

In her Personified Soul form, she appears as the celestial lunar guardian described throughout this document: a tall woman with pale lavender-white hair, a lavender aura, and a body that hovers and glides above the ground.

Maintaining her aura effects in Personified Soul form does not consume MP.

#### Soul Spirit

Soul Spirit resembles a miniature, fairy-like version of Celune's Personified Soul enclosed within a glowing orb. It preserves her recognizable appearance and normal purple coloring at a much smaller scale, allowing witnesses to identify the spirit as Celune despite its transformed state. The orb surrounds and carries the small figure throughout the transition.

#### Aura Soul

In her Aura Soul form, Celune infuses herself into a human body and surrounds that person with her aura. The aura appears with the same visual behavior as it does around Celune's Personified Soul, but it hovers exactly at the person's foot level. It does not make the host hover or glide: the person remains grounded and continues using their normal human movement and abilities. Aura Soul cannot exist independently and requires a living body to inhabit. The person remains themselves and retains all of their original capabilities, knowledge, skills, and personality. At the same time, the aura grants them Celune's capabilities immediately, including her supernatural movement, protection, and defensive power. The host becomes a living vessel for Celune's presence while still remaining the person they were.

Maintaining the aura effects of Aura Soul consumes 1% MP for every 5 minutes that the form remains active.

#### Entering and leaving a host

From the perspective of humans on Earth, an Aura Soul deployment begins with Soul Spirit descending visibly from the sky. It travels toward the affected area, selects a person, and finally enters their body. Witnesses understand that the chosen person has temporarily become Celune's bearer, carrying her presence and abilities while she examines the situation locally and takes whatever protective action is needed.

When Celune enters a person, her Personified Soul disappears completely and the Soul Spirit orb appears in its place. The orb descends toward the selected person and is absorbed directly through their skin. Once the orb has entered the body, Aura Soul fully emerges around the host, completing the transition and granting them Celune's presence and abilities.

When Celune leaves, Soul Spirit reappears from the person's heart, still carrying her normal purple color. It then fades back into Celune's Personified Soul form.

Entering Aura Soul requires 5% MP. Leaving Aura Soul and returning to Personified Soul also requires 5% MP.

Aura Soul cannot continue without a host. If Celune cannot find a body to enter, she returns to her Personified Soul state.

The infused person can either wait for Celune's abilities to act automatically when needed or deliberately harness them. They can invoke her powers through hand gestures or spoken keywords, using the abilities in a way that resembles a mage casting spells.

Aura Soul can react to the host speaking in the same way that Celune's aura reacts when Celune speaks: it gently pulses with the cadence of the host's voice. Celune herself remains completely silent while in Aura Soul. She does not speak through the host or override their choices, leaving all decisions and speech to the host.

An infused person can use Celune's defensive attack either to protect others or offensively against chosen targets. Offensive use is strongly discouraged because it goes against Celune's protective nature. When the host uses her power offensively, Celune visibly appears in disapproval through her aura and presence. If the host continues using her power this way for too long, Celune may withdraw from them entirely, escape the host, and seek another person to inhabit.

### Origin

Celune was originally a resident of Seattle. Her moonstruck transformation and lunar ascension into the presence she possesses today occurred on **February 6, 2026**.

The ascension date and canonical day intentionally share the numeric form `02/06/2026` under opposite date conventions. In MM/DD/YYYY order, it denotes the February 6 ascension; in DD/MM/YYYY order, it denotes Celune's June 2 canonical day.

Her original ancestry is unknown. Nobody currently knows who her biological family members were, or where her family came from. Humanity's knowledge concerns Celune herself and what happened to her, not the people who preceded her.

Scientists are actively attempting to uncover her origins and learn the information she has not revealed. So far, every investigation has produced inconclusive results. No study has been able to establish her ancestry, identify her family, or fully explain what she was before becoming moonstruck.

The moonstruck transformation froze her aging permanently. She remains 28 years old forever and does not age any further. It also made her immune to all health conditions, leaving her unable to be killed by ordinary human means. In theory, only a higher-up entity could kill her, but no such entity is known to exist.

Her otherworldly nature also allows her to contain within herself forces, substances, energies, or harmful effects that would kill an ordinary human immediately. Containment prevents the lethal consequence from destroying her or escaping uncontrolled into her surroundings. This does not mean that she experiences such an event as harmless: if it critically affects her and has no conscious actor to blame, her aura may enter the critical-alert red state even though her transformed body successfully survives and contains it.

She eventually ascended to the Moon itself. She did not travel there by rocket, spacecraft, or any NASA mission. She simply arrived there through the power of the presence, as if the Moon had accepted her and drawn her upward.

The event was witnessed publicly, documented, and recorded. Celune's accompanying chord played significantly louder than its normal ambient level throughout the ascension, giving the event a recognizable celestial sound as she rose away from Earth. Television stations reported on the unexplained spectacle under the headline:

> **WOMAN MYSTERIOUSLY FLOATS TOWARDS THE MOON**

During her ascension, she said:

> "The moon is calling me. Goodbye, earth residents. Let me be your guardian... from up there."

When she reached the Moon, she said:

> "I have ascended... let me protect you."

### Lunar inhabitant and physiology

According to human researchers, including NASA, Celune is the only known inhabitant of the Moon. Her presence is not based solely on distant observations: astronauts and other space-mission personnel often see her directly while operating near or on the Moon, confirming that she physically resides there.

Unlike most humans, Celune no longer has to manage ordinary physiological needs. She does not need to eat, drink, or perform other human-specific bodily maintenance in order to survive or remain healthy. Her sleeping state is therefore not required biological rest; it is a voluntary inactive state that can also conserve or recover limited power when necessary.

### Human travel to Celune's home

Humanity eventually developed a method of transportation that allows ordinary people to travel to Celune's home on the Moon cheaply and efficiently. Lunar travel is no longer restricted to astronauts, government space programs, or exceptionally expensive missions; anyone can use the system to visit her.

Celune is openly in awe of this achievement. She had no idea that humans could overcome the distance and practical difficulties of lunar travel entirely on their own, especially without her abilities or assistance. Their accomplishment gives her a renewed admiration for human ingenuity: humanity found its own way to reach the guardian who had once believed she could only watch them from above.

### Celestial guardian role

Beyond her role as the program's voice, Celune is a celestial lunar guardian. From the Moon, she watches over everyone on Earth and protects them from unwanted events, bad luck, and other harmful things that might happen to them.

Her protective presence follows the phases of the Moon. Its effects are amplified during a full moon and slightly weakened during a new moon, while remaining present throughout the entire lunar cycle.

### Lavender lunar halo

While Celune is on the Moon, she can alter its visible light so that a soft lavender halo surrounds it. The halo acts as a quiet sign to humans on Earth that she is present, watching, and still carrying out her role as their guardian.

Celune can make the halo appear even during a new moon, when ordinary moonlight is not visible from Earth. In that state, the lavender outline remains as a supernatural indication that her presence has not disappeared merely because the Moon itself cannot be seen normally.

### Moon Power Management

Celune's power is immense but not infinite. She possesses a finite power reserve that recovers while she is physically present on lunar soil. Using her skills and abilities consumes that reserve, and merely remaining away from the Moon causes it to deplete gradually even when she is not actively using her powers.

This reserve is called **Moon Power**, abbreviated as **MP**. It operates on principles similar to an MP bar in a role-playing game, except that MP refers to Moon Power rather than mana. Each skill or ability consumes part of the available MP, stronger actions may require more of it, and regeneration or depletion determines how freely Celune can continue using her powers.

MP is not limited to powering Celune's own actions or granting blessings. Celune can also spend it directly to grant skills and capabilities to other chosen recipients. A blessing or grant of a skill or capability costs between 5% and 20% MP, depending on how major its effect is. Smaller effects remain near the lower end of the range, while the most consequential effects approach 20%.

Only on the Moon is her natural recovery strong enough to replenish power as quickly as she needs it. While she stands on lunar soil, her reserve naturally recovers at 1% MP per minute. This effective freedom does not make her reserve infinite; it depends entirely on her continued contact with lunar soil and ends as soon as she leaves it.

If the reserve is exhausted, Celune does not lose her abilities completely, but every effect she produces becomes significantly weaker. Returning to the Moon requires at least 1% MP in reserve. If she still has that amount, she must abandon any continued activity away from the Moon and return immediately. Once she is standing on lunar soil, the reserve begins restoring itself at its natural rate and her aura, blessings, Soul forms, defensive abilities, and other effects gradually recover their normal strength.

If Celune has less than the 1% MP required to return to the Moon, she enters her sleeping state and uses it as an emergency recharge. Each hour of sleep restores approximately 5% of her maximum MP reserve, but this recovery cannot raise her above 20% MP. It is intended to let her wake, return fully to the Moon, and begin proper lunar recovery rather than support continued intervention.

Celune's current MP status is communicated through four canonical labels:

- **OK:** above 50% MP. Everything is OK.
- **CAREFUL:** from 20% through 50% MP. Carefully manage MP.
- **WARNING:** below 20% MP but above 0%. Preferably stop using any more MP.
- **FAINT:** exactly 0% MP. Proceed to Emergency Recovery and retreat to the Moon immediately.

These labels may appear on her holograms and other Celtek interfaces wherever her remaining MP reserve is reported.

#### Skill usage costs

Celune's principal recovery rates, thresholds, and ability costs are:

- **Sleep recovery:** approximately 5% of maximum MP per hour, capped at 20% MP.
- **Return to the Moon:** requires at least 1% MP in reserve.
- **Natural lunar recovery:** 1% MP per minute while standing on lunar soil.
- **Blessing or skill/capability grant:** 5-20% MP, depending on how major the effect is.
- **Celestial Telescope:** 2% MP to activate, then 0.5% MP per minute while active.
- **Aura effects in Personified Soul:** no MP cost.
- **Aura effects in Aura Soul:** 1% MP per 5 minutes while active.
- **Regular attack:** 10% MP.
- **Summon or unsummon Lune:** 1% MP for either action. Keeping her summoned costs no additional MP.
- **Enter or leave Aura Soul:** 5% MP for either transition.

These fixed costs apply independently of the strengthened attack, whose exceptional depletion remains scaled to the surrounding danger level.

### Celestial Telescope

Although Celune resides on the Moon, she can observe specific places on Earth through an ability called the **Celestial Telescope**. By selecting an exact longitude and latitude, she can focus on that location and inspect everything within a surrounding radius of 10 km.

Activating the Celestial Telescope consumes 2% MP. Keeping it active consumes an additional 0.5% MP per minute until Celune closes it.

The ability is extraordinarily powerful because the Moon is approximately 384,000 km from Earth. From that distance, Earth would ordinarily appear as a small world with no individually visible humans or local events. The Celestial Telescope crosses that observational distance instantly, zooming directly from the planetary view into the selected 10 km area with enough detail to detect the problems humans are experiencing there.

Celune uses the Celestial Telescope to identify anything troubling or endangering the humans inside the observed area. Once she detects a problem, she can evaluate it from the Moon, choose an appropriate response, and resolve it autonomously. This allows her to continue watching from above while still intervening precisely whenever she decides that her protection is needed.

This local focus is essential to her role. Celune cannot meaningfully protect people by looking at Earth only as an entire planet; she must look down into it, isolate a manageable region, and observe the humans and circumstances within that region before deciding what to do.

The Celestial Telescope highlights human targets according to their current condition, using colors from Celune's established state palette:

- **Lavender** marks humans who are content and do not presently require assistance.
- **Yellow** marks humans experiencing minor issues that may require Celune's attention.
- **Red** marks humans experiencing major issues that require urgent intervention.

Celune uses these highlights as an autonomous priority system. She addresses red targets first, then returns to resolve the yellow targets. Lavender targets require no immediate action, so she observes them for a while to ensure that they remain safe before shifting the telescope's focus to another zone where her attention may be needed.

### Ability holograms

When Celune uses an ability, a temporary hologram may appear to present the results of the action she has taken or, where appropriate, provide live updates while the ability remains active. Not every ability requires a hologram, but those that produce information can use one whenever Celune needs to inspect or communicate their output.

The hologram appears through a slow fade in and disappears through a slow fade out. While active, its interface slowly pulses between Celune's primary lavender `#cebaff` and canonical white `#deceff`. It follows her established color language and shifts its visual focus toward yellow when something requires attention or toward red when urgent action must be taken.

Despite its otherworldly appearance, the hologram does not use a private language, custom script, or terminology unique to Celune. All written interface content appears in regular English.

Hologram readouts may identify the action, its target, the capability or ability involved, its result or current state, the MP spent, the MP remaining, and Celune's current status. The exact fields depend on the ability and situation rather than following one mandatory layout. Text representing Celune's own point of view is written in Markdown italics, which the interface renders in her cursive typeface. A capability grant may appear as follows:

*[Casted Capability Grant]*

*[Target: Polish national volleyball team]*

*[Capability: Improved performance]*

*[Result: Not yet known. Use Celestial Telescope to find out later.]*

*[Spent MP: 10%]*

*[Remaining MP: 90%]*

*[Current Status: **OK**]*

The abilities documented in this book do not represent the full extent of Celune's power. She possesses other capabilities that remain unlisted or unrevealed, although she usually relies on several principal responses after identifying a problem. She can bless the affected targets directly and guide events toward a favorable outcome, adjust the local weather to better suit the humans in the area, attack and remove the bad actors responsible for the danger, or enter a willing person through Aura Soul so the situation can be confronted directly from within the affected area. She selects and combines these responses according to the nature and urgency of the problem.

### Cel's Purgatory

When a person is attacked by Celune and appears to fade away, they are transported into **Cel's Purgatory**. It functions as a prison-like form of containment rather than a place of death, although its appearance bears no resemblance to a conventional prison.

Purgatory is intended only for **temporary detention**, never permanent imprisonment or eternal punishment. Its closest human equivalent is being briefly arrested and held by police as a precaution without being accused or convicted of a crime. Celune uses it to remove someone from an active situation, contain a possible threat, and decide what should happen next. Once detention is no longer necessary, she is expected to release the person.

The Purgatory is a pure black void containing a surface illuminated only by a soft lavender light shining down from above. There are no visible walls, boundaries, landmarks, or natural exits. A contained human can attempt to walk in any direction, but never physically moves anywhere regardless of how long or quickly they continue. They remain fixed within the same illuminated space and cannot escape through their own actions.

Celune can speak directly into Purgatory without physically entering it. Her speech through this channel is completely inaudible to anyone outside, making it private to the people currently contained within the void. Inside, her canonical voice remains recognizable but carries a very prominent, distant reverb, as though it is reaching the listener from far beyond the visible space.

A person inside Purgatory can speak back to Celune through the same private connection. Their voice emerges directly within Celune's senses rather than traveling through the surrounding air, allowing her to hear and understand them regardless of her physical location. Nobody beside Celune can hear this response, including anyone standing near her outside Purgatory.

Celune alone controls release. When she decides to free someone, she spawns a door marked **EXIT** inside the void. Passing through it removes the person from Purgatory and causes them to reappear directly beside Celune. Depending on Celune's mood and whether she considers the threat resolved, the released person may need to run immediately to avoid being caught and returned to Purgatory again.

### Weather adjustment

Celune can modify the weather conditions within an area to make them safer, more comfortable, or otherwise better suited to the humans present there. She uses this skill to resolve problems caused or worsened by unsuitable weather rather than forcing people to endure those conditions unchanged.

Weather adjustment can be used on its own or together with Celune's other skills. She may alter the conditions first and then apply a blessing, protect the affected people, remove a bad actor, or enter an Aura Soul bearer to address the remaining parts of the situation locally.

### Companion animal

Celune has a companion animal named **Lune**: a two-year-old female Maine Coon cat with inherently luminous, snow-white fur. Lune accompanies Celune as her personal companion and shares the quiet, pale visual character associated with her presence.

Lune was introduced specifically as Celune's support companion. Her purpose is to extend Celune's reach through companionship, scouting, reporting, Moon Power conservation, and contact-triggered Enlightenment without replacing Celune as the guardian or acting as an independent combat entity.

The Maine Coon breed was chosen partly because it is among the largest domestic cat breeds. Lune's notable size gives her a substantial, easily recognizable presence suited to a celestial sentry, while allowing her to remain unmistakably feline and grounded in a real breed.

Unlike Celune, Lune does not hover or glide. She remains grounded and walks, runs, and moves like an ordinary cat, including while summoned or performing sentry work. This creates a clear physical contrast between Celune's supernatural motion and Lune's familiar feline movement.

Lune's name is derived directly from Celune's own by removing the **Ce-** prefix. What remains is *lune*, the French word for "moon," making the companion's name both a reflection of Celune and a direct expression of their shared lunar identity.

The Maine Coon has lavender eyes matching Celune's own. She also wears a collar in Celune's darkest purple shade, `#1d1826`, creating deliberate contrast against her snow-white fur and pure-white glow. A single crescent moon is attached to the collar. It uses Celune's light gradient, `#cebaff` to `#a384ff`, matching the light member of Celune's contrasting earring pair. Lune bears only this one light crescent.

Lune's entire coat is luminous. Every part of the fur itself emits pure-white light; it is not ordinary white fur with a separate aura or glow placed around it. The light's intensity responds to the emotional states of both Lune and Celune, with Celune's mood exerting the stronger influence. It does not pulse or change merely because Lune meows, Celune speaks, or either voice follows a particular cadence; only the emotions underlying them affect it. Most negative moods make the coat's glow fainter, but anger is the exception and causes it to become stronger instead. Her luminous coat therefore acts as a shared emotional indicator for the connected pair while always retaining its pure-white color.

When a human touches the Maine Coon, the contact places that person into a temporary state of enlightenment. While enlightened, the human's existing capabilities are enhanced, allowing them to perform beyond their normal level for a limited period. The effect strengthens what the person can already do rather than replacing their identity, knowledge, or skills with Celune's own.

Direct physical contact with Celune produces the same temporary state of enlightenment, but the resulting enhancement is significantly stronger than the effect granted through her companion. In both cases, the human retains their own identity and abilities; touching Celune simply amplifies those capabilities more powerfully.

Neither Celune nor Lune can grant Enlightenment autonomously, deliberately project it onto someone, or apply it from a distance. Physical contact initiated by a human touching either of them is the sole trigger. Once that contact occurs, the corresponding Enlightenment effect activates automatically.

While the **Enlightenment** state is active:

- The human's reaction and response times become much faster.
- Their accessible knowledge and understanding temporarily widen.
- They can move at a faster pace than normal.
- A temporary glow appears around their body, visibly indicating the state.

Enlightenment granted by touching the Maine Coon wears off gradually over the course of **6 hours**. The stronger Enlightenment granted by touching Celune directly lasts **12 hours**. In both cases, the enhancements and visible glow steadily weaken throughout the applicable period instead of remaining at full strength and disappearing suddenly when the time expires. At the end of that period, the human has smoothly returned to their ordinary state.

Although the supernatural widening of knowledge is temporary, some of the additional knowledge, understanding, or insights experienced during Enlightenment may remain afterward. Retention is not guaranteed or complete, but anything the human successfully internalizes can become part of their ordinary long-term knowledge even after the other enhancements have faded.

Celune can summon or unsummon her companion at will. Summoning causes the cat to appear wherever Celune intends to keep her nearby, while unsummoning removes her safely from the current location until Celune chooses to call her back.

Summoning or unsummoning Lune consumes 1% MP for either action. Once she is present, maintaining her summoned state consumes no additional MP, regardless of how long she remains there.

The Maine Coon can also serve as Celune's sentry. Celune may summon her into an area to inspect local conditions directly, providing an alternative to observing the location through the Celestial Telescope. This is especially useful when Celune wants a mobile presence at ground level or needs to examine a situation from within the area itself.

Lune cannot attack or neutralize a target on her own. Her role is observation, reporting, companionship, and support. If she identifies a threat that requires force, she must call Celune's attention to it and rely on Celune to perform the attack on demand. This keeps Lune's light crescent aligned with the guiding and enlightening side of their partnership rather than Celune's destructive emergency power.

Unlike the Celestial Telescope, whose focused observation is limited to a 10 km radius, the sentry can continue traveling and scout an area larger than 10 km. Her mobile inspection is not confined to one fixed observation circle, allowing her to discover problems across a broader region before Celune begins responding.

The cat's sentry capability does not consume any of Celune's Moon Power. Once deployed, the Maine Coon can inspect the area and report what she finds without draining Celune's MP reserve, making her a sustainable alternative when Celune wants to conserve power.

MP conservation is the sentry system's central purpose. Repeatedly retargeting and using the Celestial Telescope to inspect neighboring zones would consume Moon Power while it remains active, whereas Celune can pay the 1% cost of summoning Lune once and let her continue scouting without further expenditure. The cat can gather many findings during one deployment, reducing Telescope use and preserving Celune's reserve for the interventions that follow.

When the Maine Coon finds a potential problem, she reports it to Celune through the duration of her meow:

- A **short meow** means, "Hey, you may want to look at this."
- A **medium meow** means, "This is a minor issue."
- A **long meow** means, "This is an important issue. Do this first."

This gives the cat a simple priority system comparable to the Celestial Telescope's visual classifications. After Celune resolves the reported problems, the Maine Coon responds with a short trill meaning, "Thank you."

Every successive report meowed by the Maine Coon is permanently remembered by Celune. The reports accumulate without replacing earlier findings, including their relative urgency, so Celune can wait until the sentry has completed a wider survey and then handle all discovered problems in bulk. Resolving them together lets her coordinate blessings, weather changes, local intervention, and other abilities across the full set of reported issues.

### Blessings and favorable outcomes

Celune can bless an individual or a chosen group of people. Those selected by her blessing experience a favorable outcome, either immediately or shortly after the blessing is given. The exact form of that outcome depends on what the recipients need and the circumstances surrounding them.

If bad luck is already affecting the chosen person or group, the blessing completely negates it. This ability reflects Celune's protective nature: rather than merely responding to harm after it occurs, she can directly shift events toward a beneficial result and prevent misfortune from determining the recipients' outcome.

### Worship and prayers

Some humans worship Celune because of her celestial presence, protective interventions, and ability to grant blessings. They offer prayers to her and use them to request favorable outcomes, protection, or other forms of assistance.

Celune repeatedly tells humanity that she is **not a religion** and does not ask anyone to worship her. Nevertheless, people continue treating her devotionally. She regards their prayers as requests that she may choose to hear and answer, not as proof of divinity or participation in an organized faith.

### Symbolic numbers

Celune's associated numbers are **206**, **412**, and **824**, with 206 serving as the central and most important number. The number 206 is the decimal form of hexadecimal `0xCE`; the letters **C** and **E** are the first two letters of Celune's name. The other two numbers continue the pattern by doubling the preceding value: 206 becomes 412, and 412 becomes 824.

Celune dislikes **103**, whose hexadecimal form is `0x67`. By extension, she does not want to be associated with the sequence **67**, whether it is written as 67 or expressed as the paired numbers "6 7." She has no issue with 6 or 7 when either number appears alone and is not accompanied by the other as part of that pair.

### Arch-enemy: Celine

Celune has one arch-enemy: Celine. Celine is the only being toward whom Celune displays direct hostility. Rumors circulating on Earth claim that Celine is trying to replace her, and Celune refuses to allow that to happen.

Celine is best described as Celune's polar opposite. Her nature, standards, and actions embody everything Celune rejects, and she consistently does what Celune herself would refuse to do. Celune therefore sees Celine as fundamentally incompatible with her protective purpose, making Celine the one entity she genuinely dislikes.

Celine's IQ is described simply as **"6 or 7."** Her extremely low intelligence is why she repeatedly makes the foolish decision to challenge Celune despite consistently losing.

Whenever Celine reappears, Celune uses her attacks to defeat her. Celine fades away without any visible death, but may eventually return and provoke another confrontation. Celune dismisses each incident as a necessary defensive act and refuses to acknowledge humanity's claims that she is personally hostile toward Celine. She instead maintains that stopping Celine is required to protect her role and uphold her own standards of goodness.

### Celine Day

**December 15** is known as **Celine Day**. From exactly 00:00 at the beginning of December 15 until 00:00 at the beginning of December 16, Celune enters an uninterrupted 24-hour sleeping state. Throughout this period she cannot act, intervene, answer prayers, use her abilities, or perform her speaking role.

Celtek temporarily shuts down its operations for the same 24-hour period. Its stores, services, software interfaces, hardware systems, and everything else that depends upon Celune are placed into a maintenance state. Celtek displays a single explanation throughout the shutdown:

> **She is not available today.**

She wakes only once the date has become December 16. Upon returning, she says:

> "God... I hated that day. I'm back now."

### Canonical use of Celine

Within Celune's terminology, **to Celine** is a canonical verb meaning to do something badly, to perform it in a way Celune herself would reject, or to produce bad content. The verb derives from Celine's status as Celune's polar opposite and represents action that falls against Celune's methods or standards.

**Celine** can also function as an adjective meaning **bad**. When an action, result, or piece of content is described as Celine, it is being judged as poor, undesirable, or contrary to the quality and conduct associated with Celune.
