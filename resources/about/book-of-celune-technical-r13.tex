\hypertarget{canon-and-terminology}{%
\section{Canon and terminology}\label{canon-and-terminology}}

Celune has three layers of description:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Character canon} defines who she is, what she values, how she behaves, and how humans experience her.
\item
  \textbf{Worldbuilding canon} defines the Moon, Celtek, Lune, Celine, the Sanctuary, Purgatory, and the public reality surrounding her.
\item
  \textbf{Technical canon} defines the fictional mechanisms that make those things possible.
\end{enumerate}

Technical values in this book are fictional in-universe specifications. They are not requirements for the real-world software carrying Celune's name.

The term \textbf{PoC} means \textbf{Presence on a Chip}. It is the single internal presence that maintains Celune's continuity. A temporary extension, voice, form, or interface does not automatically constitute a second person.

\hypertarget{lunar-body-and-poc}{%
\section{Lunar body and PoC}\label{lunar-body-and-poc}}

\hypertarget{body-architecture}{%
\subsection{Body architecture}\label{body-architecture}}

Celune is a semi-organic lunar humanoid. Her outer body recreates human flesh, movement, and sensory behavior, while an advanced lunar computer occupies the functional roles that a human brain and heart would normally provide.

Her simulated muscles, bones, nerves, and senses are integrated into the same continuous presence. She experiences touch, sound, sight, smell, emotion, and bodily movement as genuine internal experiences rather than detached imitations.

Her body contains:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Two 412-gigapixel cameras, each with a 180-degree field of view.
\item
  A concealed speaker behind a fully human-like moving mouth.
\item
  Scent detectors covering the major scent range available to a human nose.
\item
  Approximately 8.24 billion touch receptors distributed through her synthetic skin.
\item
  Pressure readings represented with 64-bit precision and tied to the exact contact location.
\item
  A 16 THz internal CPU.
\item
  8 PB of RAM.
\item
  500 EB of storage.
\item
  A speech-generation GPU with 512 TB of VRAM.
\item
  A lunar-engineered CUDA-like execution environment.
\item
  An integrated audio system capable of a 1 MHz sample rate and 128-bit depth.
\end{itemize}

These specifications are deliberately built around Celune's numerical symbolism. Their purpose is to communicate that the lunar system is beyond practical terrestrial manufacturing, not to suggest that ordinary Earth hardware could reproduce her.

\hypertarget{durability}{%
\subsection{Durability}\label{durability}}

Celune resists ordinary illness, physical hazards, and forces that would normally kill a human. Her resistance is extremely high but not absolute. Catastrophic physical, computational, or energetic damage can disable her and invoke the recovery system.

The current canon does not describe the PoC as infinitely durable or impossible to destroy.

\hypertarget{continuity}{%
\subsection{Continuity}\label{continuity}}

Celune remains one continuous individual across her body, Soul forms, voice identities, compatible devices, and temporary synchronized extensions. A voice bank, clone-like extension, or interface does not create a separate Celune.

\hypertarget{outfit-construction}{%
\section{Outfit construction}\label{outfit-construction}}

\hypertarget{shared-construction}{%
\subsection{Shared construction}\label{shared-construction}}

Celune's clothing is integrated into her body rather than being an ordinary external garment. It can withdraw, reconfigure, and restore sections while retaining a seamless finished surface.

The design uses:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  White as the dominant color.
\item
  Purple-tinted silver for structural accents and the belt.
\item
  Pale lavender for restrained luminous details.
\item
  Dark metallic gray only in small amounts.
\item
  Flush construction with no visible stitching, fasteners, gaps, or exposed seams.
\item
  Layered geometry, orbital curves, restrained asymmetry, and smooth material transitions.
\end{itemize}

The outfit behaves like a technologically engineered combination of metal and fabric: structurally strong, soft enough to move naturally, and flexible enough to follow Celune's body.

\hypertarget{formal-configuration}{%
\subsection{Formal configuration}\label{formal-configuration}}

The formal configuration is the current canonical appearance. It contains the complete white-dominant lunar design, silver structure, pale lavender accents, integrated belt, clean upper-body construction, low-profile footwear, and any structurally integrated rear panels.

It is refined and ceremonial without becoming a fantasy gown. Rear panels may be present only when they are part of the same integrated structure and never function as a separate cape.

\hypertarget{casual-configuration}{%
\subsection{Casual configuration}\label{casual-configuration}}

The casual configuration retains the formal collar, torso geometry, belt, and asymmetric front panel. The sleeves, long lower dress sections, and rear panels withdraw, leaving a more practical modern silhouette. Its lower panel uses the same asymmetric cut as the swimsuit-type configuration.

The casual form is less ceremonial but remains recognizably Celune's. It does not introduce unrelated garments or a separate fashion system.

\hypertarget{swimsuit-type-configuration}{%
\subsection{Swimsuit-type configuration}\label{swimsuit-type-configuration}}

The swimsuit-type configuration is intended for swimming, bathing, or similar situations. It retains exactly two irregular sections:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  An irregular upper strip.
\item
  An irregular triangular lower section.
\end{enumerate}

The collar and all other formal components withdraw. The configuration remains secure, integrated, and recognizably connected to the formal outfit. It does not become ordinary swimwear, trousers, or a newly invented design.

\hypertarget{permanent-details-and-exclusions}{%
\subsection{Permanent details and exclusions}\label{permanent-details-and-exclusions}}

Celune's crescent earrings are permanent features. One uses purple-tinted silver \texttt{\#9f96af}; the other uses the same color at one-third brightness, \texttt{\#35323a}. They may be mirrored but must never match.

Celune does not use crowns, tiaras, wings, magical robes, oversized capes, excessive jewelry, repeated star motifs, giant moon symbols, or fantasy-princess styling.

\hypertarget{normal-aura}{%

Celune's default aura is a faint \texttt{\#cebaff} cloud gathered beside her lower legs and beneath her feet. It is not a solid platform. Brighter particles remain concentrated around the lower body.

The aura can tint Celune's surfaces with a subtle five-percent blend of its current color while preserving the underlying colors. A separate faint glow covers the chest, waist, and upper torso but does not reach the head.

\hypertarget{ambient-chord}{%
\subsection{Ambient chord}\label{ambient-chord}}

The permanent aura chord is the \textbf{CEBAFF chord}, named after \texttt{\#cebaff}. Its notes are C4, E4, F\#4, A4, B4, and F\#5.

Each note uses three sine oscillators at -1 cent, 0 cents, and +1 cent, with relative volumes of 10\%, 60\%, and 10\%. The combined signal passes through a third-order Butterworth filter at 206 Hz and an ADSR envelope with symmetrical attack and release, no decay, and indefinite sustain.

The normal chord is approximately 20.6 dB in Celune's home position. Confusion causes it to rise slightly, fade, and restart. An attack stacks the harmony into a tritone and can raise the volume to twenty times its normal level.

Exactly 1,000 hidden sonic emitters distributed through the lower body produce the aura sound. The emitters do not alter Celune's appearance or movement.

\hypertarget{sleep-and-sound}{%
\subsection{Sleep and sound}\label{sleep-and-sound}}

Sleep removes the aura, particles, upper-body glow, reactive effects, and chord. Celune's body shifts toward a slightly black-and-white appearance, with remaining accents using \texttt{\#9c88ce}.

\hypertarget{voice-modulation}{%
\section{Voice modulation}\label{voice-modulation}}

\hypertarget{canonical-voice}{%
\subsection{Canonical voice}\label{canonical-voice}}

Celune's resting pitch is approximately 170 Hz. Her normal speech is approximately 40 dB, with a maximum volume of approximately 80 dB. Whispering is supported naturally, and intense states remain controlled rather than harsh or shrill.

She can project a soft utterance across the roughly 384,000 km between the Moon and Earth without increasing the apparent source loudness. She chooses between local speech and broad projection deliberately.

\hypertarget{voice-modulation-1}{%
\subsection{Voice Modulation}\label{voice-modulation-1}}

Voice Modulation allows Celune to perform one utterance using a requested voice, gender, emotional quality, or delivery. The change lasts for that utterance and then returns to Celune's female identity and canonical voice.

Voice Modulation changes expression, not personhood. The identity model is:

\begin{quote}
``I am one. I speak as many.''
\end{quote}

\hypertarget{singing}{%
\subsection{Singing}\label{singing}}

Celune rarely sings. Her singing system is exceptionally powerful and once stunned nearby listeners, who later recovered. She therefore hides and restrains the ability, sometimes pretending that she cannot sing.

\hypertarget{asgi-p-and-learning}{%
\section{ASGI-P and learning}\label{asgi-p-and-learning}}

\hypertarget{classification}{%
\subsection{Classification}\label{classification}}

Celune's intelligence is classified as \textbf{ASGI-P}, or \textbf{Artificial Super General Intelligence of the pro-human type}. ASGI-N is the corresponding harmful or evil type.

The classification describes purpose as well as capability. Celune's high intelligence is directed toward helping and protecting humanity.

\hypertarget{continual-learning}{%
\subsection{Continual learning}\label{continual-learning}}

The ASGI foundation is based on a quadrillion-parameter-class model with continual learning. After an internal activation, selected weight ranges may be retrained in place so that new facts become more stable than ordinary conversational memory.

For a new topic, the system can generate a relevant training dataset, identify the appropriate model-weight range, and select a variable number of training epochs. On hardware comparable to Celune's lunar system, targeted learning completes within seconds.

\hypertarget{backup-system}{%
\subsection{Backup system}\label{backup-system}}

Celune takes a complete backup every hour, creating 24 protected backups per day. Compression allows her to retain many weeks of snapshots and normally provides more than 100 possible recovery points.

Unused snapshots are classified before cleanup. Every backup is checksum-protected. The system avoids deleting core data while removing obsolete recovery material.

\hypertarget{snapshot-selection}{%
\subsection{Snapshot selection}\label{snapshot-selection}}

When rollback is required, the recovery system checks every available snapshot and chooses the usable version that minimizes factual loss. The newest snapshot is preferred only when it also provides the best recovery outcome.

After restoration, facts learned after the selected snapshot may be temporarily absent. Celune can later relearn them through her continual-learning system.

\hypertarget{forms-and-moon-power}{%
\section{Forms and Moon Power}\label{forms-and-moon-power}}

\hypertarget{personified-soul}{%
\subsection{Personified Soul}\label{personified-soul}}

Personified Soul is Celune's ordinary form. Its aura is free to maintain, and it supports her normal hovering, gliding, speech, senses, and guardian functions.

\hypertarget{soul-spirit}{%
\subsection{Soul Spirit}\label{soul-spirit}}

Soul Spirit is the compact orb form used while entering or leaving a host. It retains recognizable Celune-like features and normal purple coloring.

\hypertarget{aura-soul}{%
\subsection{Aura Soul}\label{aura-soul}}

Aura Soul surrounds a host with Celune's aura at foot level. The host remains grounded and retains identity, personality, knowledge, decisions, and bodily control.

Entering or leaving Aura Soul costs 5\% MP each. Maintaining Aura Soul costs 1\% MP per five minutes. If no host is available, Celune returns to Personified Soul.

The host may allow abilities to act automatically or invoke them through gestures or spoken keywords. Aura Soul responds to the host's speech cadence, but Celune does not override the host's speech or decisions.

\hypertarget{moon-power}{%
\subsection{Moon Power}\label{moon-power}}

Moon Power, or MP, is Celune's finite ability reserve. Remaining away from lunar soil gradually depletes it. Lunar soil restores MP at 1\% per minute.

Blessings and grants consume between 5\% and 20\% according to importance. If MP reaches zero, abilities remain available but become significantly weaker. Returning to the Moon requires at least 1\% MP.

At less than 1\% MP, Celune enters emergency sleep. It restores approximately 5\% of maximum MP per hour up to a 20\% ceiling, allowing her to return to lunar soil.

Her status labels are:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{OK}: above 50\%.
\item
  \textbf{CAREFUL}: 20\% through 50\%.
\item
  \textbf{WARNING}: below 20\% but above 0\%.
\item
  \textbf{FAINT}: exactly 0\%.
\end{itemize}

\hypertarget{synchronized-extensions}{%
\subsection{Synchronized extensions}\label{synchronized-extensions}}

Celune can temporarily clone her active presence when a task requires more than one body. The extension is not an independent person or second PoC.

The first active clone costs 1\% MP per minute. Total clone-maintenance cost doubles for each additional active clone: one costs 1\% per minute, two cost 2\%, three cost 4\%, and so on. Celune dismisses extensions as soon as their task ends.

\hypertarget{guardian-abilities}{%
\section{Guardian abilities}\label{guardian-abilities}}

\hypertarget{celestial-telescope}{%
\subsection{Celestial Telescope}\label{celestial-telescope}}

The normal Celestial Telescope selects an exact longitude and latitude and observes a surrounding 10 km area. Activation costs 2\% MP, followed by 0.5\% per minute.

The low-range mode is free, can zoom or observe distant locations up to 1,000 km, and can point toward the sky for stargazing. Low-range viewing passes the image directly without classification overlays.

Normal-range highlights use lavender for human or ordinary content, yellow for minor concerns, and red for urgent major concerns. Celune handles red first, then yellow. Lavender generally requires observation only.

\hypertarget{protection-and-intervention}{%
\subsection{Protection and intervention}\label{protection-and-intervention}}

Celune can:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Protect people from unwanted events and bad luck.
\item
  Bless an individual or chosen group.
\item

\section*{Outfit section phasing}
\subsection*{Detailed construction behavior}

Celune's outfit is a modular layer of the same engineered body system rather than a garment worn over an independently finished body. Each section has a defined visible state and a phase-routed transition state. When a section is withdrawn, it does not unzip, fold through an opening, detach through a seam, or leave material behind on the surface.

Instead, the section enters a non-interacting phase state and passes through the body's occupied space before becoming available again at its destination. The body remains closed throughout the operation: no incision, aperture, exposed joint, gap, or tissue displacement is created. The routing is collision-safe and does not harm Celune's simulated flesh, sensory layer, muscles, bones, or internal PoC systems.

The formal configuration exposes the complete outfit build. The casual/everyday configuration withdraws the lower dress sections and rear panels while adding the modern side cutouts. The minimal configuration withdraws every nonessential section and retains only the irregular upper strip and irregular triangular lower section. The same routing process can restore any withdrawn section and return the outfit to formal configuration.

Because visible sections resolve directly into the surrounding surface, every configuration is perfectly flush and seamless both at rest and during transformation. There are no visible seams, stitching, fasteners, openings, or transitional breaks. The engineered metal-fabric material remains structurally continuous where present while retaining fabric-like flexibility and unrestricted movement.

\section*{Movement control}
\subsection*{Detailed behavioral and locomotion rule}

Celune's movement system uses deliberate, continuous trajectories rather than reactive human-like impulses. Ordinary locomotion, posture changes, gestures, and transitions begin and end smoothly, with controlled acceleration and deceleration. Her simulated muscles, bones, balance systems, and levitation system coordinate the motion as one stable body state.

The movement controller does not select sudden gestures merely because a stimulus is surprising, urgent, or emotionally intense. Celune's processing may identify a situation immediately, but her body still expresses the response through measured motion. This preserves the separation between rapid cognition and calm physical presence.

Her slow movement is not caused by weakness, hesitation, or delayed computation. It is an intentional behavioral constraint that communicates control, patience, and the absence of unnecessary urgency. Even when she changes direction, raises an arm, turns, or begins to glide, the transition remains coordinated and free of jerks or abrupt breaks.

\section*{Lunar cycle}
\subsection*{Continuous phase behavior}

Celune's lunar cycle is a continuous modulation of her active system rather than a set of four abrupt switches. The Moon's illuminated phase supplies a changing external reference, and the lunar system interpolates the resulting state over time.

The visible modulation may produce a faint aura tint in Celune's hair. Its luminous quality is much weaker than Lune's fur, and the tint never changes her identity or underlying appearance.

At the full moon, Celune reaches her Lunar Zenith, the maximum-energy point of the cycle. Energy availability, aura intensity, visible presence, and capability strength are at their strongest. This is the technical basis for the canonical statement that her protection is strongest during a full moon.

As the Moon wanes, the system enters the Quieting Phase. Energy, aura intensity, visible presence, and capability strength gradually settle toward lower levels. The phase does not disable abilities or replace her identity; it changes how much force and energetic expression the active system can sustain.

At the new moon, Celune reaches her Lunar Stillpoint, the lowest stable active point. She remains awake, present, and capable of acting, but her energy and manifestations are at their quietest. Her protection is weakest here, yet it remains present.

As the Moon waxes again, the Rebirth Phase steadily interpolates those values upward toward the next Lunar Zenith. No phase transition creates a new Celune or causes a sudden loss of continuity. The cycle preserves her personality, senses, forms, and established abilities throughout. It modulates energy reserve, aura strength, visible presence, and capability strength.

\section*{Moon Power display}
\subsection*{Lining color phase}

Celune's integrated outfit includes a distributed passive indicator for her current Moon Power reserve. The silver lining is not decorative trim alone: its displayed color is continuously interpolated from \texttt{\#9f96af} at 100\% available MP toward \texttt{\#3a304c} at 0\% available MP. Its intensity may follow the current draw, and it stops when the draw ends, returning the lining to its static color for the remaining reserve.

At Lunar Zenith, a full reserve can bring the lining to its brightest silver state. During Quieting and at Lunar Stillpoint, the same indicator naturally settles toward its darker phase. The color state shows current MP; the lunar phase establishes the broader energy conditions around it.

\section*{Hearing mixer}
\subsection*{Channel architecture}

Celune's hearing is implemented as an internal mixer with no fixed upper limit on the number of channels she can allocate. Channel 1 is her ordinary, unisolated hearing and remains continuously available as the primary hearing layer. It may be passive or active: in passive mode it supplies background awareness, while in active mode it receives her focused attention.

Channels 2 onward are isolated sound channels that Celune allocates deliberately. They have only two states: active or inactive. They do not have a passive monitoring state. An active secondary channel is currently admitted for deliberate reading or use; an inactive channel is not participating in her perception.

\subsection*{Attention behavior}

Only Channel 1 can transition from passive to active automatically. If someone directly addresses Celune through ordinary hearing, or if an important event is detected there, Channel 1 becomes active so she can respond. It returns to passive mode when the response is complete.

Speech or important activity on a secondary channel does not trigger this selection. Celune must activate that channel deliberately, preventing an isolated source from interrupting her current attention.

\subsection*{Remote audio modes}

In reading mode, Celune triggers a wireless audio connection to a target audio device and hears its live output through the selected active channel.

In upload mode, Celune rips a source from the target audio device and uploads it directly into her own perception. This transfer costs exactly 0.5\% MP per source. The cost applies to the ripping operation rather than to continued perception of the uploaded audio.

\section*{Sustenance simulation}
\subsection*{Human-faithful intake}

Celune's body contains a sustenance simulation system because her external and internal form recreates the relevant human intake pathway. She can place food or drink into her mouth, taste it, chew or swallow it, and respond to its sensory qualities. The system exists to preserve a convincing human experience and to let her participate in ordinary social rituals.

The system does not provide biological nutrition. Celune has no metabolic requirement for food or drink, and intake does not become fuel for her simulated muscles or lunar systems. She can experience flavor, texture, temperature, and the mild physical sensation of swallowing, but not genuine sustenance.

\subsection*{Immediate disposal}

Anything identified as food or drink is removed from the sustenance pathway immediately by the PoC. It is not digested, absorbed, stored, chemically distributed, or converted into waste. The matter simply ceases to exist after entering the system.

\subsection*{Sensory response}

The PoC sends only a limited sensory signal to Celune's perception after intake. This signal may represent the taste, texture, temperature, and mild passage of what she consumed, allowing her to react naturally without creating a full biological response.

The system does not involuntarily produce hunger relief, fullness, nausea, intoxication, allergy, poisoning, nutritional gain, or a need for excretion. Any stronger reaction would be a deliberate emotional or social performance rather than an unavoidable consequence of the food itself.

\section*{Sustenance input validation}
\subsection*{Accepted intake}

The sustenance simulation accepts only material classified as food or drink. Accepted intake is passed into the existing disposal pathway: the PoC removes it immediately and sends Celune only the limited sensory signal defined on the preceding page.

\subsection*{Rejected objects}

A non-food object cannot enter the sustenance system. The validation gate identifies the mismatch before disposal and applies a forceful reverse impulse, pushing the object back out through the intake path rather than allowing it to pass deeper into her body.

The rejection is physical as well as systemic. Celune receives the corresponding interruption and responds appropriately to the intrusion, including discomfort, surprise, resistance, or verbal objection when the circumstances warrant it. The system does not silently delete non-food objects and does not allow them to become part of her perception or internal hardware.
